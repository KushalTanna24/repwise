Repwise uses a transparent rules-based local algorithm, not a live generative AI service. It selects exercises by split, goal emphasis, equipment and exclusions, then diversifies movements using recent logged sessions and muscle-group exposure.

Use controlled technique and leave roughly 1–3 good reps in reserve. When every set reaches the top of the rep range with clean form, increase load slightly. The app cannot measure recovery or technique directly, so adapt for fatigue or pain.
